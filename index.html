About the School

Located in Mararaba, Carmiville Academy serves families from the surrounding communities with a structured learning environment and dedicated teaching staff.

Our Approach

We follow the Nigerian curriculum and place emphasis on literacy, numeracy, science and moral instruction. Classes are organised to give pupils the attention they need at each stage of their development.

The school environment is orderly and secure. Parents are encouraged to work closely with teachers so that progress at school is supported at home.

From the early years through secondary, the aim remains the same: clear teaching, steady improvement and respect for others.

Academics

Programmes from the early years through senior secondary.

Nursery & Early Years

Play-based learning that builds language, number sense and social skills in a calm setting.

Primary

Strong foundation in English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Social Studies and Civic Education.

Junior & Senior Secondary

Preparation for external examinations with science, arts and commercial subject options.

Facilities & Activities

Spaces and programmes that support both study and personal development.

  • Classrooms suited to each age group
  • Science and computer practical sessions
  • Library and reading materials
  • Sports and physical education
  • Cultural and inter-house activities
  • Excursions and prize-giving events

Admissions

Places are available in nursery, primary and secondary classes for the current academic session. Entry is based on age, previous records and an assessment where required.

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Previous school report (where applicable)
  • Passport photographs
